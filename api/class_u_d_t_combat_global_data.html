◆ ArmorConstant

float UDTCombatGlobalData::ArmorConstant = 2500.0f

Controls how strongly armor reduces incoming damage.

Formula: ArmorReduction = Armor^p / (Armor^p + ArmorConstant^p) (p = ArmorExponent) ArmorConstant is the amount of armor required for 50% mitigation (at Armor == K the two terms are equal regardless of p). Mitigation does not depend on hit size and asymptotically approaches but never reaches 100%. Lower values make armor stronger.

◆ ArmorExponent

float UDTCombatGlobalData::ArmorExponent = 0.6f

Skews the armor curve.

p = 1 is linear-in-EHP (every point of armor worth the same survivability). p < 1 front-loads the curve: early armor gives much more mitigation, and the late game diminishes faster. p > 1 back-loads it (weak early, ramps later). Affects shape only; the 50% point stays at K.

float UDTCombatGlobalData::BaseBlockEffectiveness = 0.5f

Base fraction of damage removed by a successful block, before BlockEffectiveness scaling.

Formula: BlockDamageReduction = min(BaseBlockEffectiveness * (1 + BlockEffectiveness/100), MaxBlockDamageReduction)
